## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes early 20th-century luxury maritime heritage with precision predictive intelligence. It draws directly from the Gilded Age aesthetic of transatlantic first-class voyage registries, re-engineered for algorithmic data visualization, telemetry monitoring, and high-consequence predictive modeling.

### Emotional Disposition
The interface evokes:
- **Quiet Authority:** An atmosphere of established maritime command, deliberate and unhurried.
- **Architectural Permanence:** Tactile heft resembling heavy deck logbooks, calfskin manifests, and hand-tooled brass instruments.
- **Analytical Rigor:** Modern statistical readouts anchored inside classical nautical framing, avoiding hyper-digital sci-fi tropes in favor of timeless instrumental clarity.

### Visual Style
The design language adopts an **Ornate Tactile Minimalism** ethos:
- Deep oceanic nocturnal surfaces establish an abyssal baseline.
- Antiqued gold foil, warm copper hairline rules, and burnished brass accents deliver focal contrast.
- Visual elements emulate physical artifacts: engraved inspection stamps, telegraphic tick-marks, ledger rule lines, and perforated manifest tickets.

## Colors

The color palette is derived from deep-sea hydrography, engine-room brass, and first-class stateroom appointments.

### Primary Palette
- **Primary (`#C5A059` — Antique Gold Foil):** Serves as the primary focal tone for primary interactions, active navigational milestones, key metric callouts, and key-value anchors.
- **Secondary (`#1B263B` — Abyssal Slate):** Forms structural panels, elevated cards, and segmented control containers.
- **Tertiary (`#8B3A3A` — Portside Oxide Burgundy):** Applied selectively to critical alerts, confidence threshold boundaries, warning states, and anomaly detection indicators.
- **Neutral (`#0D1B2A` — Midnight Trench):** The fundamental canvas substrate across the interface, grounding all viewport environments.

### Contextual and Semantic Surface Applications
- **Surface Elevation 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0A131E` (Darker than base neutral for profound contrast).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Manifest Planks / Cards):** `#111E2E` with a hairline stroke of `#C5A059` at 18% opacity.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Inspection Drawers & Gauges):** `#162538`.
- **Text & Reading Hierarchy:**
  - High Contrast (Parchment White): `#F4EFE6`
  - Subdued Contrast (Aged Cream): `#C4BCB1`
  - De-emphasized / Telemetry Meta: `#6F7D8E`

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional tension between classical Edwardian editorial prestige and technical instruments.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`EB Garamond`):** Deployed for view headers, ledger titles, passenger/entity designations, and statistical verdicts. Headings evoke the grace of bespoke typography found on naval builder plaques and first-class saloon menus.
- **Telemetry, Controls & Running Text (`Manrope`):** Deployed across data fields, tabular coordinates, confidence intervals, and interactive inputs. Its geometric yet human proportions maintain legibility under high information density.

### Typographic Rules
- Set `label-sm` and all ledger metadata in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to mirror stamped naval plates.
- Numerical values in machine-learning readouts must employ tabular lining numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure vertical alignment across dynamic dashboard streams.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial discipline adheres to architectural maritime engineering: rigorous, symmetrically anchored, and compartmentalized like water-tight vessel bulkheads.

### Grid Construction
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid with `3rem` canvas margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Content containers conform to a maximum reading span of 1440px to retain formal balance.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Side panels compress into retractable drawer docks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column system anchored by `1.25rem` outer margins. Dashboards reflow into sequential stacked ledgers.

### Rhythm & Padding Rules
- Internal container gaps follow the `space-*` scale strictly based on an 8-point base rhythm with half-step micro tokens (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px).
- Structural ledger headers maintain double the bottom spacing relative to their intra-element field pairs to clearly distinguish narrative headings from numerical matrices.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on generic drop shadows, this design system models physical depth through **Tonal Layering, Subtle Inner Lathed Highlights, and Brass-Gilded Edge Light**.

### Layering Architecture
- **Deck Ground (Canvas):** Pure `#0A131E`. No shadow; acts as the abyssal ocean floor.
- **Stateroom Tier (Base Cards & Containers):** Layered at `#111E2E`. Bordered with a 1px solid rule of `#C5A059` at 20% opacity.
- **Instrument Tier (Active Toolbars, Floating Panels):** Layered at `#162538`. Bordered with a 1px rule of `#C5A059` at 40% opacity, supplemented by an ultra-soft ambient occlusion shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(4, 8, 15, 0.75)`.

### Tactile Edge Highlights
- Components utilize an inner 1px inset rim (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)`) across top borders to simulate polished bevels catching overhead gaslight illumination.
- Avoid diffuse colorful glow halos; depth transitions must remain hard-edged, deliberate, and structural.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a controlled, minimal corner radius to convey machine precision, shipwright timber cuts, and brass plates.

### Geometry Guidelines
- A base roundedness value of `1` yields `0.25rem` (4px) radii on all primary controls, input fields, and metric modules.
- Larger groupings (`rounded-lg`) utilize `0.5rem` (8px). 
- Roundedness never reaches full pill shapes or organic curves. Chamfered or cut-corner treatments (implemented via CSS clip-paths) may be applied to prominent boarding pass badges or verification seals to invoke clipped ticket vouchers.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary (Gilded Brass):** 
  - Background: Gradient fill from `#C5A059` to `#9E7B35`.
  - Label: `#0A131E`, set in `label-md`, uppercase with `0.08em` tracking.
  - Border: 1px solid `#E0BE7A` with top inner gold specular highlight.
  - Hover: Background shifts to `#D6B26A` with subtle expansion of the inner edge highlight.
- **Secondary (Bulkhead Outline):**
  - Background: Transparent with `#111E2E` backing.
  - Label: `#F4EFE6`.
  - Border: 1px solid rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.35).
  - Hover: Border opacity increases to 80%; background shifts to `#1B263B`.
- **Destructive (Port Anomaly):**
  - Background: Transparent with `#8B3A3A` border and crimson-tinted text.

### 2. Chips & Categorical Badges
- **Style:** Compact ticket-stub silhouette. Border radius of 2px (`0.125rem`).
- **Telemetry Tag:** Dark ocean fill (`#111E2E`) with a contrasting left-edge 2px solid indicator strip (Gold for nominal, Portside Red for anomalies, Pale Slate for historical).
- Typography: Set using `label-sm` in all caps.

### 3. Cards & Boarding Pass Containers
- **Visual Treatment:** Evokes an authentic White Star Line luxury sailing manifest combined with a machine intelligence readout.
- **Card Framing:** 1px hairline border (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)`).
- **Notched Perforations:** Optional decorative dividing line between the primary metric zone and detail list, built using an SVG dashed border with scalloped circular cutouts on lateral edges.
- Header features an ornate serif title (`headline-sm`) accompanied by a gilded tabular index identifier (e.g., `REG-1912-AI`).

### 4. Input Fields
- **Container:** Dark slate trough (`#0B1522`) with an inset depth feel (`box-shadow: inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).
- **Border:** Hairline rule of `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)` transitioning to crisp solid `#C5A059` upon focus.
- **Typography:** Value input rendered in `Manrope` Regular with `#F4EFE6`. Floating label placed above in `label-sm` uppercase `#C4BCB1`.
- **Data Affixes:** Units (e.g., `knots`, `pct`, `lat/long`) aligned to the right edge in tabular, de-emphasized brass-slate typography.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkbox:** Square frame (18px) with a 2px radius. Background `#0B1522`, border `#C5A059` at 40%. When checked, reveals an engraved gold diagonal cross or mechanical tick.
- **Radio Button:** Concentric brass nautical ring indicator. The selected state activates a solid inner gold circle centered with high contrast.

### 6. Lists & Ledger Tables
- Table header row features aged cream typography (`label-md`) anchored by a bottom horizontal hairline border accented with small brass tick points at column dividers.
- Alternating row zebra fills are omitted; rows separate via subtle horizontal rules (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.08)`).
- Hovering over a row highlights the segment in `#162538` with an illuminated gold pip on the leftmost column.

### 7. Specialized Domain Components
- **Telegraph Dial / Model Confidence Gauge:** A circular or semi-circular speedometer-style SVG gauge featuring mechanical tick marks, gold needle actuators, and Garamond confidence scores centered inside the dial core.
- **Passenger/Entity Manifest Drawer:** A slide-out panel that displays a digitized version of a stateroom slip, featuring passenger metadata, survival probability, cabin deck categorization, and luggage registry logs.
